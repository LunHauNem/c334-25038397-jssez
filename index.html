02 / Move from interest to execution

Use the SETUP framework.

A five-part checklist for shaping an expansion. Use it to structure your questions, not as a substitute for agency advice.

  1. S

    Select your zone strategically

    Match the zone's target activity to your business model and operating needs.

  2. E

    Evaluate incentives and requirements

    Check whether your activity and proposed investment meet each scheme's criteria.

  3. T

    Take the right legal steps

    Plan the business structure, registrations, licences, tax and customs compliance.

  4. U

    Utilise bilateral support

    Engage the Singapore JS-SEZ Project Office and Malaysia's IMFC-J for setup guidance.

  5. P

    Prepare your talent and supply chain

    Plan workforce mobility, training and local supplier relationships.

Framework summary: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 3. Setup details: pp. 6-7.

03 / Plan with care

Incentives need a watchful eye.

The playbook highlights potential support and the conditions that can affect eligibility. Always verify current terms.

Potential support

Know what may apply.

The playbook overview lists a 5% corporate tax rate for qualifying activities, for 10 or 15 years depending on capital investment thresholds. It also lists a 5% personal income tax rate for knowledge workers.

Sector-specific incentives, grants and facilitation support are also noted. Eligibility and conditions vary.

Check the current scheme details with MIDA and Enterprise Singapore.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 3, 6

Tax watch-outs

Map activity across both sides.

  • Permanent establishment: Where staff work, make decisions or negotiate may affect whether a company has a taxable presence in Johor.
  • Transfer pricing: Cross-border transactions between related companies should use arm's-length prices and have supporting records.
  • Staff workdays: Work in Johor can bring Malaysian tax obligations. Double-tax relief depends on specific conditions.

Track locations, duties and workdays. Get professional advice on your operating model.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 14-16

04 / Plan for people

The right pass depends on the work.

The playbook distinguishes expatriate employment from temporary project work and business travel.

For expatriate roles

Employment Pass

MIDA assesses expatriate post applications in manufacturing, selected services and approved regional or representative offices. Requirements vary by category and can change.

Check current requirements with ESD ↗ SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 28
For defined temporary work

Professional Visit Pass

The playbook describes this for temporary professional work such as training, consultancy or project work, including machine installation. It is not extendable and does not provide long-term employment rights.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 28
For foreign business travellers

Investor Pass

The playbook describes a multi-entry route for business travellers, with stays of up to 12 months in six-month periods. An extension depends on the stated requirements.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 29

Important: Improved mobility does not remove tax or immigration obligations. Confirm the right pass for each person's activities and length of stay.
